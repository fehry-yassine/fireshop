# Product Definition

## Product Name

Temporary name: LocalMarket

Candidate final name: FireShop

The final name is not locked yet, so project documentation should keep using LocalMarket until the name is confirmed.

## One-Sentence Pitch

LocalMarket helps Tunisian local merchants and small businesses sell products online by giving buyers structured category navigation, clean product discovery, trusted vendor onboarding, and a simple cash-on-delivery order flow.

## Problem

Many Tunisian local merchants sell through social media, phone calls, or physical stores, but they do not have a structured way to manage products, stock, customer orders, and delivery status in one place. Buyers also struggle to discover products clearly, compare vendors, trust sellers, and place simple COD orders.

## Solution

LocalMarket provides a shared marketplace where:

- Buyers browse multiple local product categories, add products to cart, and place COD orders.
- Vendors manage products, stock, and order preparation.
- Admins approve vendors/products and supervise marketplace activity.

## Target Users

- Buyers in Tunisia who want a simple local online shopping flow with COD.
- Local merchants and small businesses who want online visibility without building a full e-commerce website.
- Admins who need control over vendors, products, users, categories, and orders.

## Initial Categories

1. Electronics & Accessories
2. Home & Kitchen
3. Fashion
4. Beauty & Personal Care
5. Sports & Fitness
6. Baby & Toys
7. Car Accessories
8. Local Handmade

## Business Model

Primary model for V1:

- Commission per delivered order, default planning assumption: 8 percent.
- Manual featured/offers sections controlled by the admin.

Future revenue options:

- Vendor subscription plans.
- Sponsored categories.
- Delivery service partnerships.

## Value Proposition

- For buyers: easier product discovery, simpler ordering, clearer order tracking.
- For vendors: no need to build a store, centralized product/order management.
- For admins: controlled marketplace growth with approval and moderation workflows.

## Presentation Language

The presentation and report are expected to be in French, with English technical terms where necessary.
